1. What soil texture means

Soil texture is the relative proportion of sand, silt, and clay. Particle size is different from soil color, fertility, or the arrangement of particles.

SandLargest of the three particle sizes.
SiltIntermediate particle size.
ClaySmallest of the three particle sizes.
Sand % + Silt % + Clay % must equal 100%.
1

Check the total

Do not classify a sample until the three percentages add to 100%.
2

Follow each axis

Clay increases upward, sand increases toward the lower left, and silt increases toward the lower right.
3

Find the intersection

The region where the three values meet is the soil texture class.

Class triangle

↑ Clay increases upward ↙ Sand increases left ↘ Silt increases right

Packet example: 40% sand + 40% silt + 20% clay = loam.
